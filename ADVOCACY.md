**Purpose:** grassroots live music advocacy correspondence for inner-Melbourne.
**Compiled:** 29 July 2026. **All data pulled live from official sources on that date.**

## Read this before you send anything

1. **The Victorian ministry is in flux right now.** On 27–28 July 2026 Jacinta Allan lost
   the support of the Labor caucus and **Ben Carroll was elected the 50th Premier of
   Victoria**. Sources: [Thank You, Victoria (Allan, 28 Jul 2026)](https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/thank-you-victoria-0)
   and [Statement From The Premier (Carroll, 28 Jul 2026)](https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/statement-premier-170).
   A new ministry will almost certainly be sworn in within days. **Re-check every Victorian
   ministerial portfolio in section 3 immediately before writing.** The Parliament of Victoria
   members database was mid-update when this list was compiled and its ministry data is
   demonstrably incomplete (no Treasurer or Creative Industries Minister listed).
2. A Victorian state election is imminent — the new Premier's own statement refers to
   "this election". Expect MPs to be highly responsive to local advocacy right now, and expect
   electorate offices to close during the caretaker period.
3. **Verify each address at send time.** Emails were read verbatim off official pages; they
   were not pattern-guessed. Nothing marked `UNVERIFIED` should be invented.
4. Suburb-to-district mapping below was resolved with the Victorian Electoral Commission's
   own boundary API at each suburb's approximate centre. **Suburb boundaries and electoral
   boundaries do not align** — a venue on the edge of a suburb may fall in the neighbouring
   district. Check any specific address at
   <https://www.vec.vic.gov.au/electoral-boundaries/which-boundaries-cover-where-i-live>.
