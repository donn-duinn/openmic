## The finding that matters most

**Of roughly 26 comedy rooms, only three publish a usable room email.** Every
other way in is a Facebook message, an Instagram DM, a Google Form or a
clipboard. Comedy in the Cellar runs a *lottery* on a Google Form that closes at
8pm the day before.

That is the pain, documented with sources. It is also why comedy may be the
better market than music.

**Two people are worth more than most venues.** Jarrod Kane (`admin@deadfunny.club`)
runs Bar Humbug *and* deadfunny.club, a live directory of Melbourne rooms that
names who runs each one — a distribution partner, not just a customer. Joel
Temperly and Michael Haber each run two rooms.
