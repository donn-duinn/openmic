# Plain-English agreements for musicians

Free to use, copy and adapt. CC BY-SA 4.0.

**Read this bit first.** These are templates, not legal advice, and nobody here
is your lawyer. If a gig involves real money or you are being asked to sign
something you do not understand, join Musicians Australia (MEAA) and use the
contract help you get as a member. That is what it is for.

**The single most useful thing in this document** is not any template. It is
this: *get it in writing before you play.* A text message saying "confirming
$300, 9pm, 45 min set, you provide PA" is a contract. A handshake in a loud
room is not. Most disputes in grassroots music are not people being dishonest.
They are two people who remember a conversation differently, six weeks later,
with no record.

---

## 1. Before you say yes — the checklist

Ask all of these. If the answer to any is a shrug, get it in writing before you
commit.

- **How much, and when am I paid?** On the night, or in 30 days?
- **Is it a fee, a door split, or both?** If a split, what percentage, and split
  of what — gross door, or after costs?
- **If it's after costs, which costs, and how much are they?** Get numbers, not
  categories. This is where most people lose money.
- **Am I expected to sell tickets?** If yes, and it affects whether you get paid
  or keep your slot, that is pay-to-play. You can say no.
- **What time do I load in, soundcheck, and go on? What time do I finish?**
- **Is there a PA and someone to run it? Or am I bringing gear?**
- **Can I sell merch, and does the venue take a cut?** They shouldn't.
- **What happens if it's cancelled?** By them, or by you.
- **Who's on the bill with me, and in what order?**

**The number for context:** Musicians Australia sets a minimum of **$250 per
musician** for a gig up to 3 hours. If you are offered less, that is your
decision to make, but make it knowing the floor exists.

## 2. Template — booked gig

Copy this into an email. Send it after the conversation. If they reply "yep",
you have an agreement.

> Hi [NAME],
>
> Confirming tonight's details so we're both across it:
>
> **Gig:** [ARTIST NAME] at [VENUE], [DATE]
> **Fee:** $[AMOUNT] total, for [NUMBER] musicians
> **Paid:** [on the night, in cash / by bank transfer within X days]
> **My ABN:** [ABN, or "no ABN — I'll provide a Statement by a Supplier form"]
>
> **Times:** Load in [TIME], soundcheck [TIME], on stage [TIME], off [TIME]
> **Set:** [LENGTH] minutes, [NUMBER] sets
>
> **Venue provides:** [PA, engineer, backline, drum kit — list it]
> **I provide:** [list it]
>
> **Merch:** I'll be selling merch and keeping 100% of it.
> **Cancellation:** If the gig is cancelled with less than [7] days' notice,
> the fee is still payable. If I have to cancel, I'll give as much notice as I
> can and help find a replacement.
> **Recording/filming:** Any recording or filming of my set for the venue's use
> needs my okay first.
>
> Let me know if I've got anything wrong.
>
> Cheers, [NAME]

**On the ABN line:** if you don't quote an ABN, a business paying you generally
has to withhold 47% for tax. If you don't have one and this is a hobby rather
than a business, ask them for a "Statement by a Supplier" form. Talk to an
accountant, not to us.

## 3. Template — door split

The one that goes wrong most. The whole trick is agreeing the costs *before*,
in numbers.

> **Gig:** [ARTIST] at [VENUE], [DATE]
> **Door price:** $[X] per head
> **Split:** [X]% to artists, [X]% to venue
> **Split is calculated on:** [gross door takings] / [door takings after the
> costs listed below, and no others]
> **Agreed costs, if any:** sound engineer $[X], door person $[X].
> Total $[X]. No other deductions.
> **Guarantee:** artists receive the greater of $[X] or their share of the door.
> **Door count:** I can see the door count / clicker total at the end of the
> night.
> **Paid:** on the night.

That "and no others" clause is the entire point. Without it, costs appear after
the show that nobody mentioned before it.

## 4. Template — open mic slot

Open mics are casual and should stay casual. But if a venue is running one with
any kind of pool or payment, this is the whole agreement:

> **Walk-ins play free.** Turn up, put your name down, play. No cost, no
> quota, no obligation.
> **Performers who play get:** [a drink / $X / a share of the jar]
> **The tip jar** is split evenly between everyone who played that night. The
> venue takes none of it.
> **If there's a pool:** [how it's funded] split between performers who signed
> up in advance and brought people.
> **Nobody is ever required to sell or buy tickets to keep a slot.**

## 5. Template — sound engineer and photographer

The people most often expected to work for exposure. Same rules apply.

> **Engagement:** [NAME] to [run sound / photograph] at [VENUE], [DATE]
> **Times:** [in] to [out]
> **Fee:** $[AMOUNT], paid [when]
> **Credit:** [NAME] is credited by name on the venue's sign-up page, the stage
> display, and in any press or social posts using the work.
>
> **Photographers only — copyright:** [NAME] owns the copyright in all images.
> The venue may use them for [social media and promotion of this venue] with
> credit. Any other use, including press or paid advertising, needs separate
> permission. This licence can be withdrawn.

**Credit is not payment.** It goes on top of a fee, never instead of one. If
someone offers you exposure as the fee, that is the industry's oldest line and
you are allowed to say no.

## 6. If a gig goes wrong

- **You weren't paid:** put it in writing, politely, with the agreed terms
  attached. Most of the time this resolves it, because most of the time it's
  disorganisation, not theft.
- **Still not paid:** Musicians Australia (MEAA) will help members chase it.
  Victoria Legal Aid and community legal centres also exist.
- **Small amounts:** VCAT handles small civil claims in Victoria and is designed
  to be used without a lawyer.
- **You're struggling:** Support Act Wellbeing Helpline, **1800 959 500**, free,
  24/7, for anyone working in Australian music.
- **You need LGBTIQA+ specific support:** Rainbow Door, **1800 729 367**, or
  text 0480 017 246, 10am to 5pm daily.
