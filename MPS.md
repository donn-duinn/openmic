# Parliamentary Representatives — Greater Melbourne

**Compiled:** 29 July 2026
**Purpose:** verified contact list for real correspondence (Open Mic / live-music advocacy).

## Accuracy statement — read this first

- **No email address in this document was pattern-guessed.** Every state email, phone and
  address was read directly from that member's own page on `parliament.vic.gov.au`
  (scraped 29 July 2026). Every federal email, phone and address was read from the
  official *List of Members, House of Representatives, 48th Parliament, Vol 10.3*
  published by the Parliament of Australia.
- Anything not confirmed from a primary source is marked **UNVERIFIED**.
- Capitalisation of emails is reproduced as published (e.g. `Melissa.Horne@...`).
  Email is case-insensitive on the local part in practice, so this does not matter for sending.

## Corrections to the brief

Three assumptions in the original brief are wrong, and matter:

1. **There is no "Eastern Metropolitan" Legislative Council region.** Victoria's five metro
   regions are Northern Metropolitan, **North-Eastern Metropolitan**, Southern Metropolitan,
   South-Eastern Metropolitan and Western Metropolitan. This document uses the real names.
2. **Greater Melbourne is not 38 state districts — it is 63** (plus 3 peri-urban fringe
   districts listed separately). All 63 are included below. Victoria has 88 districts total.
3. **Victoria has a new Premier.** Jacinta Allan's premiership ended in July 2026;
   **Ben Carroll (ALP, Niddrie) became Premier on 28 July 2026** and **Gabrielle Williams
   (ALP, Dandenong) became Deputy Premier on the same day**. Both dates are read verbatim from
   their parliament.vic.gov.au position histories. As at the time of compiling,
   `premier.vic.gov.au` had **not** been updated and still presented Jacinta Allan — do not
   rely on that site this week. Ministerial titles below are taken from members' own
   position histories, which carry commencement dates; some may still shift in the days
   after a leadership change.

### The gap worth naming in your letter

A full-text scan of all 128 sitting Victorian MPs' pages found **zero occurrences of
"night-time economy"** or "24-hour economy" — in any biography, any portfolio title, any
position history. **No Victorian minister, shadow minister, parliamentary secretary or
commissioner has responsibility for the night-time economy.** New South Wales created a
24-Hour Economy Commissioner; Victoria has not. That absence is a concrete, verifiable ask:
*someone should own this.*
